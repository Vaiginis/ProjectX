1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a site asks your browser to store. Similar technologies — local storage, session storage and tracking pixels — do much the same job, and this policy covers those too.

Cookies let a site remember things between page loads: that you are signed in, which answers you gave, or which language you chose. They can also be used to measure how a site is used and to show advertising.

3. What we do with what cookies collect

Data from strictly necessary cookies is used only to operate the site and is not shared for marketing.

Data from the other categories is processed on the basis of your consent, and in some cases is collected directly by the provider who sets the cookie, acting as controller or joint controller. Those providers are listed in our Privacy Policy. Where such data leaves the EEA, the safeguards described there apply.

We do not use cookie data to make decisions that produce legal effects for you.

4. Changing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your choices at any time from the cookie settings link in the site footer. Withdrawing consent stops further collection; it does not undo processing already carried out lawfully.

You can also manage cookies in your browser — every major browser lets you block or delete them, and offers a private browsing mode that clears them at the end of the session. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop parts of the site working, including sign-in and checkout.

Note that browser controls apply per browser and per device, so you will need to repeat them elsewhere.

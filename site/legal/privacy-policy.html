2. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

Visiting the site

When you open the site we process your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you view and how you arrived. We do this to keep the site working and secure, on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating it. Analytics and advertising data are processed only with your consent — see the Cookie Policy.

Taking the quiz

The answers you give in the onboarding quiz are stored in your browser and, once you create an account, linked to it. We use them to assemble the learning plan you asked for, which is performance of our contract with you.

Creating an account

We process your email address and password (stored only as a hash), and any name you choose to give. This is necessary to provide the account, which is performance of our contract.

Providing the Services

We process your progress, exercise answers, project submissions and the messages you send to tutors or the community, in order to deliver the Services and show you your own progress. This is performance of our contract.

Payments

To take payment we process your billing email, country, the plan you bought and the outcome of the transaction. Card details go directly to our payment provider and we never see or store the full number. This is performance of our contract and, for tax records, compliance with a legal obligation.

Support and communications

When you contact support we process what you send us and our replies, so we can help you and keep a record of the issue. This is our legitimate interest in running a support function. Marketing emails are sent on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We may disclose personal data where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Where we are permitted to tell you about such a request, we will.

6. How we protect your data

Technical measures. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored hashed and salted. Access to production systems requires individual accounts and multi-factor authentication, and is logged. Backups are encrypted.

Organisational measures. Access is granted on a need-to-know basis and reviewed regularly. Staff are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive data protection training. We maintain a procedure for handling personal data breaches and, where required, will notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours.

7. Your rights

Where the GDPR or a comparable law applies to you, you have the right to:

  • be told whether we process your data, and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data deleted where we no longer have grounds to keep it;
  • have processing restricted while a dispute about it is resolved;
  • object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests, including profiling;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these, email help@tryivio.com. We will respond within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to your local supervisory authority.

8. Children

The Services are not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Where data is transferred out of the EEA or UK, we rely on an adequacy decision where one exists, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses together with any additional safeguards the transfer requires. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.
